🧀 Cheese Chase

A wheel of cheese. A ridiculously steep hill. Absolutely no dignity.

Run / steer WASD or arrowsBrake / steady S / ↓ Sprint hold Shift (risky!)Jump Q Dive SpaceGrab the cheese E Grab a racer F (slows / trips them)Quit Esc · Rematch R

Catch the cheese (get close and press E) or be first over the finish line to win. The hill speeds you up on its own; sprinting builds speed fast but makes you far likelier to trip. Nobody lands a jump or dive on their feet here: you ragdoll and roll, then get up (press Space once you've slowed).
2-player: P1 = WASD, Left Shift sprint, Q jump, Space dive, E grab cheese, F grab racer · P2 = Arrows, Right Shift / Num0 sprint, ' / Num3 jump, Enter dive, . / Num1 grab cheese, / / Num2 grab racer.
An original game inspired by the traditional cheese-rolling races of Gloucestershire.
