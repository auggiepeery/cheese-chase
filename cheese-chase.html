🧀 Cheese Chase

A wheel of cheese. A ridiculously steep hill. Absolutely no dignity.

Run / steer WASD or arrowsBrake S / ↓ (calms your balance) Sprint hold Shift (2.2 s, refills when released)Jump / dive Space Grab E (the cheese if close, else a racer)Mid-tumble Space = roll-jump (2 charges) / get up Sound M muteQuit Esc · Next heat R

Catch the cheese or be first over the line. Best of 3 heats. Rolling down the hill is fast: tumble, then press Space to launch yourself at the cheese and steer in the air. Jump near the cheese and you dive at it automatically (a dive that reaches it grabs it). A well-timed jump on smooth ground lands clean with a speed boost; a bad one sends you rolling. Too much speed or wild steering fills your balance meter.
2-player: P1 = WASD, Left Shift sprint, Space jump, E grab · P2 = Arrows, Right Shift / Num0 sprint, Enter jump, / or . / Num1 grab.
An original game inspired by the traditional cheese-rolling races of Gloucestershire.
